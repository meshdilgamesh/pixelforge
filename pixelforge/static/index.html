Welcome to PixelForge

Blurry in. Crystal out.

Take a low-quality image, a compressed JPEG, or an AI render and forge it into a sharp, detailed, high-resolution version — up to 8K. It runs 100% on this computer: nothing is ever uploaded anywhere. New here? This 30-second tour is all you need.

Step 1

Feed it an image

Drag & drop anywhere on this window, or click the drop zone to pick files. You can throw a whole folder of images at it — PixelForge processes them one by one. The fuzzier the source, the more dramatic the reveal.

WebPhoto · great default DAT-2 · max detail Anime · for art
Step 2

Choose your edge

Each model is a different "forging style". You can't go wrong with the default (WebPhoto) — sharpest detail and fastest. Feeding a clean AI image and want everything it can give? Pick DAT-2. Anime or game art? There's a model for that too. Every card says what it's best at.

Step 3

Pick a size · Forge ✦

4× is a great start, or switch to Exact resolution for 1080p / 1440p / 4K / 8K / any custom size — the aspect ratio is always preserved. Hit ⚡ Forge it, then drag the divider on the reveal to compare, and press Save. That's the whole craft.
